Good Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you enquire. Anything else — just ask us on WhatsApp.

Is the first demo class really free?

Yes. Share the student's class and subjects (or the course you want), and we arrange a demo class — at home or online — so you can judge the teaching before enrolling. No payment is taken for the demo.

What are the fees for home tuition?

Indicative monthly fees: Nursery–Class 1 ₹1,500 · Classes 2–4 ₹2,500 · Classes 5–7 ₹2,500+ · Classes 8–10 ₹3,500–₹4,000. Final fees depend on class, subjects, location and tutor availability, and are confirmed before you start.

How much time does my child study each day?

Typically around 1 hour per day, with Sundays off. Exact timing and schedule are confirmed individually to fit your family's routine.

We don't live in Ranchi or Lohardaga. Can we still join?

Yes — all tuition and courses are available live online, anywhere in India. For home tuition we currently serve Ranchi, Lohardaga, and nearby areas including Khunti and Chatra.

Do the career courses include practical work?

Yes — every course includes practical assignments. Digital Marketing runs as a 1.5-month internship program, and Data Analytics includes projects plus internship / placement assistance as applicable.
